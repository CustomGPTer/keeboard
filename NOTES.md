KeeBoard 0.1.0 beta 2, a test build.

KeeBoard is an offline keyboard for Android. It has no internet permission, no account, no adverts and no tracking.

What is in this test build:
- QWERTY with UK and US English typing dictionaries, word completions and spelling suggestions. Auto-correction is off until you turn it on.
- Glide typing (off by default), spacebar cursor control, swipe back to delete a word, and accents and symbols on a long press.
- 3,963 built-in emojis with search, plus original GIFs and stickers kept on the phone.
- Offline English voice typing. Nothing is recorded to a file.
- Optional clipboard history that clears itself after an hour, with pinning.
- One-handed, split and floating layouts, adjustable height, colours, a background picture, and themes you can save and share.
- Text shortcuts, an editing panel, and an encrypted backup file for your settings and words.

Changes since beta 1:
- Every keyboard panel now stays above the navigation buttons or gesture bar, in portrait and landscape.
- The empty suggestion row folds away, so the keyboard sits lower.
- Installs over beta 1 and keeps your settings.

Android 10 or later. Permissions: microphone (voice typing only) and vibration.
