KeeBoard cannot go online

The best-known Android keyboards do a great deal, much of it online. KeeBoard does less, and does all of it on your phone. This page sets it beside two of them, using only what their own Google Play listings say.

What KeeBoard does not do

KeeBoard types only in English, has no handwriting, no translation, no online GIF search and no syncing between devices. It is a test build, it is not on Google Play, and it cannot update itself, so you install new versions from this site.

Where the others do more

Gboard's listing describes handwriting, emoji and GIF search, many languages at once and translation. SwiftKey's listing describes over 700 languages, 100 themes and predictions that learn from you. Both are on Google Play.
