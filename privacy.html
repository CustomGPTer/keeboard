Privacy policy

KeeBoard has no internet permission, no account and no servers. The developer receives nothing from it.

In short

The developer collects nothing. KeeBoard has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This website has none either.

What you type

What you type goes to the app you are typing into, as with any keyboard. KeeBoard uses it on the phone to suggest words and keeps nothing from it unless you add a word to your dictionary or turn on clipboard history. Password fields are treated as private: no suggestions, clipboard or media tools appear in them.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, personal words, shortcuts, emoji history, clipboard history if you turn it on, and any pictures you import. They are kept in KeeBoard's private storage, left out of the phone's own backup, and removed if you uninstall the app. Privacy, Reset private data clears them at any time.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone, KeeBoard listens and turns speech into text on the phone. It keeps no recording. Listening stops when you stop it, when you leave the text box, or after two minutes.

Permissions

KeeBoard asks for the microphone, for voice typing, and vibration, for key presses. It does not ask for the internet, your contacts, your location, your files or accessibility access.

Backup files

A backup file is made only when you ask for one. It is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and you decide where it goes. The passphrase cannot be recovered.

Children

KeeBoard is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
